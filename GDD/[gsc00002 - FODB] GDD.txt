OVERVIEW
* The game is a fast-paced, Asian-themed Mine-style gamble title designed around an intuitive grid-reveal mechanic for instant, high-stakes decision-making.
* Incorporating the iconic legends of the Four Divine Beasts transforms traditional mine gameplay into a culturally rich treasure hunt that drives strong player resonance and high retention across Asian markets.
* Combining a lightweight mobile-first engine with progressive payout multipliers ensures seamless, lag-free performance on all devices while delivering an exhilarating risk-and-reward experience.

BASE GAME
* A Board composed of 16 tiles (lotus buds) serves as the playing area for the game.
* To begin, press the "Start" button. Once initialized, select a tile on the grid; this action will reveal either a Gem or nothing.
* If the tile shows a Gem (golden lotus), the main multiplier advances by one level (also for Sacred Jade), giving you the option to either continue playing or Cash Out.
   * Cash Out: You receive the winnings based on the current multiplier level, then the round ends.
* The player can choose multiple tiles in a short period of time, with limitation of 2 tiles at a time. After selection, these tiles will be revealed in chosen order and the result will be shown.
* Revealing a tile with nothing (withered lotus) results in an immediate loss, concluding the round with no reward.
* The round's difficulty is fully adjustable with 1, 2, or 3 empty tiles, mapping directly to Easy, Normal, and Hard levels.
* Sacred Jade: Special Sacred Jade may be found when revealing tiles. Collecting these and making combos will give a bonus multiplier, and also gives you a chance of earning a bonus reward.

COMMON RULES
* A round is defined as the entire period from the moment a player initiates the bet by pressing "Start" until the final payout outcome is determined (either the bet is lost or the winnings are credited back to the wallet).
* A Block is defined as the entire period, from the moment a player chooses to open a tile until the resulting animation finishes (which could be a multiplier increase, a crash, or a Bonus animation).
* Each round comprises multiple blocks. Successfully completing a block advances the multiplier by one increment.
* A round of the game concludes with one of two outcomes: Cash Out or Crash.
Cash Out: A player manually ends the round by pressing the Cash Out button, resulting in the winnings being transferred to their wallet.
Crash: The round ends immediately, and no winnings are received for that round, when a Crash tile is revealed.
* Upon reaching the maximum multiplier level, the player's round will conclude with an automatic Cash Out.

AUTOPLAY MODE
The player can enable Auto-play mode, where the game runs automatically based on pre-set configurations Auto Mode Configuration


The player must define the following settings before starting Auto Mode:
         * Bet Size: The bet size is fixed based on the currently selected bet and cannot be changed while Auto Mode is active.
         * Difficulty: Specifies the difficulty of automatic rounds to be played.
         * Number of Auto Rounds: Specifies the total number of automatic rounds to be played.
         * Number of Empty Tiles Each Round: Defines the number of empty tiles present in each auto-play round.
         * Auto-Select Tiles: Specifies the number of the tiles/cells that the game will automatically select in each round.
         * Selection Requirement: At least one tile must be selected to initiate Auto Mode, the number will start as 1 when users first open the mode. By pressing the button to select, the value will increase or decrease by 1 tile. Users can hold the button or scroll for fast selection.
         * Selection Limit: The maximum number of selectable tiles is based on the difficulty for the round. (Ex: you can only select up to 13 out of 16 tiles when setting Hard difficulty)
         * Limit Handling: If the number of Crash tiles increases after the maximum tile selection limit has been reached, the last selected tile will be automatically deselected.
         * Reset Option: A "Reset" button is available to deselect all tiles, resetting the selected tile back to 1. It is enabled when the number of selected tiles are from 2 or more.
         * Selection Order: The tiles will be selected in the random order for each round when Autoplay runs.
  

Winning Chance Display


A label displays the calculated Winning Chance, which dynamically updates according to the current game setup (bombs and auto-selected tiles).
         * Example: For a setup of 3 bombs and 1 auto-selected tile, the winning chance is displayed as 79.62%.
         * Formatting: The displayed value is rounded to a maximum of two decimal places (e.g., 4.567% is displayed as 4.57%).
         * The Winning Chance formula is calculated as follows:
         * N = Total number of tiles
         * J = Total number of bombs
         * N - J = Total number of safe tiles
         * Step k = Winning Chance of successfully reaching step k
  

         * If the player has not selected a betting tile, the Winning Chance will be displayed as "---".
Starting and Stopping Auto Mode
         * Start: After configuring all settings, press the "Start" button to begin Auto Mode with the first automatic round.
         * Stopping: Auto Mode will stop at the conclusion of the current round.
Early Cash Out in Auto Mode


Even when in Auto Mode, the player can choose to Cash Out earlier than the pre-set auto-selection.
         * Effect: An Early Cash Out ends the current round immediately, and the game proceeds to the next scheduled auto-round.
         * Example: If the player sets up 6 auto-selected tiles but cashes out after the game plays 3 tiles, the current round ends, and the next auto-round begins immediately.
POWER MODE
         * Instant Resolution: Once activated alongside the Auto-play settings, this mode bypasses the standard animations to deliver final round results immediately.
         * High-Efficiency Gameplay: Designed for players seeking maximum speed, allowing multiple rounds to be processed in seconds without the need to monitor the live gameplay.
         * Seamless Integration: Works in tandem with pre-set Auto-Cashout limits, providing a streamlined and data-efficient experience for rapid-fire sessions.
Setup Steps
         1. Adjust Auto-Play Parameters: Set the base stake and determine the total number of rounds for automation.
         2. Mandatory Auto-Cashout: Configure the number of tiles to be opened; this is a mandatory requirement for Power Mode to function.
         3. Toggle Power Mode: Switch the feature to ON to initiate immediate round resolutions.
         4. Begin Session: Tap "Start" to execute the automated cycles and view instant outcomes.
         5. Popup Confirm: After pressing "Start", a Popup will appear for the player to confirm the information. The Popup will display the current configurations set by the player, including: Number of Rounds - Number of tiles to open - Difficulty - Bet Amount. Once the player confirms, Power Mode will be activated.




Operation
         * Round Tracker: Monitors the session progress by updating the count as each automated cycle concludes.
         * Cashout Target: Adjust the number of tiles required to open. Once the configured number of tiles has been successfully opened, the game will automatically trigger a Cashout.
         * Real-Time Multiplier Feed: Updates instantly to show round results through various transitions:
         * Progress Tracker: A condensed visualization of the active round, illustrating the player's real-time advancement through the session.
         * Sequential Activation: The selected Tiles will be revealed simultaneously. If at least one Crash tile is revealed among the opened Tiles, the session will result in a Lose. If all revealed Tiles do not contain a Crash tile, the player will achieve a Win.
         * Payout Multiplier: Displays the winning Multiplier obtained at that Step.
         * Bonus: Displays the Multiplier value gained from the number of collected Jade Combinations.
         * Consistent Win Feedback: Should the result remain identical across rounds, a "Flash Animation" confirms the win
         * Performance Indicators: Provides a live tally of total successful cashouts ("Wins") versus losses ("Crashes").
Instant Termination: A dedicated "Stop" button is available for players to cancel the session at any moment.




Step-by-Step Flow
         1. Upon initiating the session, the cycle count progresses linearly from the first round toward the designated limit.
         2. In the Power Mode Playing Popup, at each round, all tiles will be revealed within 1 second to instantly display the results. When transitioning to a new round, all tiles will flip back face-down, then the new round will begin and continue by revealing all tile results again within 1 second.
         3. Successful completion of a movement increment results in a corresponding boost to the current payout level.
         4. The session terminates under these conditions:
         1. Victory: If all tiles are revealed and none of the selected tiles contain Crash tile, the round is considered a win. Upon winning, the multiplier value will fade in and fade out over 0.5 seconds before proceeding to the next round.
         2. Loss: Encountering a crash before the target increment is achieved halts the cycle and adds to the loss tally.
         5. Following round completion, the subsequent cycle initiates after a 0.5-second delay, with values resetting to zero.
         6. If the player triggers a manual stop during a cycle, the current round will be completed and Power Mode will immediately terminate afterward.
         7. A session summary popup appears instantly once the stop command is executed.
  

Power Mode Result
         * A summary overlay is presented upon the conclusion of all automated rounds or a manual intervention.


         * This results screen highlights essential data, including the total accumulated prize across all completed sessions.
         * Players are then prompted with two options: exit the mode entirely or resume automation using the existing settings.
         * The total number of Jade Combinations completed will also be included in the total win count.
  

PROMOTION
A promotion is a gift from the publisher to the player, offering a set number of free rounds with a predetermined bet value.


Promotion Rounds
         * Cost: Do not deduct the bet amount from the player's account.
         * Functionality: Operate the same as standard rounds (e.g., manual play, auto-play).
         * Visibility: Included in the bet history.
         * Validity: Have an expiration date set by the publisher.
Promotion Claim and Notification
         * Popup Notification (Standard): "You have been gifted {1} free plays (bet amount {2})"
         * Popup Notification (Daily): "You have been gifted {1} daily free plays (bet amount {2})"
Claim Flows
         * Offline Claim: Player enters the game to Notification popup is displayed to Player presses 'Confirm' to In-game screen appears with free rounds ready.
         * Online Claim: Player completes the current round (if one is in progress) to Notification popup is displayed to Player presses 'Confirm' to In-game screen appears with free rounds ready.
In-Game Status when Promotion is Active
         * Start Button: The 'Start' (bet) button displays the number of remaining free rounds instead of the standard text.
         * Betting Field: Shows the value of the free round. Players cannot change the bet size while a promotion is active.
         * Activating Autoplay with Promotions: Players may engage autoplay even while a promotion is active. During this, any promotional free plays are processed as standard turns. Should the configured number of auto rounds exceed the available free plays, the system will seamlessly transition into regular autoplay rounds using the player's balance until the session is manually terminated.


COMMON BEHAVIOR (INACTIVITY):
AFK from Session Start (Step 0)
         * If the player starts a session but does not reveal any tile within 60 seconds, a warning toast will appear and flash 2 - 3 times prompting them to take action at the second of 40th, accompanied by a 10-second countdown after that (warning that a random tile will be revealed otherwise).
         * Once the 10-second countdown ends, the game will automatically reveal a random tile.
         * Toast Content:
AFK from Step 1 Onwards
         * If the player is at any step other than Step 0 and does not reveal another tile within 60 seconds, a warning toast will appear and flash 2 - 3 times prompting them to take action at the second of 40th, accompanied by a 10-second countdown after that (warning that a random tile will be revealed otherwise).
         * Once the 10-second countdown ends, the game will automatically cash out at the current step, collect the corresponding multiplier, and end the session.
         * Toast Content:
Reconnection & Resume Logic
         * Resuming in less than 60 seconds (reconnect / switch tab / minimize / login on another device): The inactivity timer will reset.
         * Resuming after 60 seconds:
         * At Step 0: The game will automatically execute a random tile reveal.
         * From Step 1 onwards: The game will automatically execute an auto-cashout.
